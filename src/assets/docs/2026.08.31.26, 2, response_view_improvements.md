**Layout and scrolling.** The app is locked to the viewport. Mechanical and recorded views fill the remaining space and scale down with their SVGs until they hit a 12rem floor. Only after that does a scrollbar appear — and it belongs to the workbench (plus the sidebar controls), not the browser window.

**Visualization controls.** The spring / translate / scale / rotate / opacity toolbar now sits at the bottom of the mechanical (or scale, etc.) panel, not across both columns.

**Auto-update.** Recorded response has an **Auto-update** checkbox. When it is on, changing engine, preset, or any parameter replays the animation and records a new curve. The setting is stored in `appSettings` and survives reloads. It defaults to on; turning it off restores manual Run / Replay.

**Graph points.** Sample dots are larger (`r=10` in SVG units) and use the graph `viewBox`, so they grow and shrink with the plot.
